Tuna Mayo Rice Bowl

Home

Ingredients

  • 1 (5-ounce) can tuna (preferably any variety stored in oil), well drained
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1 cup cooked rice (preferably short or medium grain, I use Nishiki)
  • toasted white or black sesame seeds, furikake or chopped scallions, for topping (optional)

Preparation

  1. In a small bowl, stir the tuna, mayo, sesame oil, and soy sauce to combine
  2. Add the white rice to a bowl and spoon the tuna mixture on top<, sprinkle with sesame seeds, furikake or scallions, if using/li>
