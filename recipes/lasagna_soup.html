Lasagna Soup

Description

A simple one-pot soup that delivers the classic flavors of lasagna with very little work.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1/2 pound ground beef
  • 1/2 pound bulk sweet italian sausage
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper, plus more to taste
  • kosher salt, and freshly ground black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 (24-ounce) jar marinara sauce
  • 8 ounces dried lasagna noodles, broken crosswise into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 1/2 cups/12 ounces whole-milk ricotta
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, torn, plus more for serving

Preparation

  1. In a large dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium. Add the onion and cook until translucent, but not browned (4-6 min). Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds to 1 minute, until fragrant.
  2. Add the beef, sausage, oregano, nutmeg, crushed red pepper, and some salt and black pepper. Cook and break up the meat until starting to brown (3-5 minutes), then adding the tomato paste and cooking for an additional minute, stirring often.
  3. Add the chicken broth and marinara sauce, bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Stir in lasagna noodles, then reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, uncovered, for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. While the soup simmers, combine ricotta and parmesan in a medium bowl, add a pinch of salt and black pepper and mix well; set aside.
  5. Turn off the heat, stir the cream and basil into the soup, then taste and add more salt and crushed red pepper if desired.
  6. Serve the soup in a shallow bowl, topping with a dollop of the ricotta mixture. Add basil to garnish.
